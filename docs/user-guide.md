# FMI{Codes} 2026 User Guide

This guide explains how to use the FMI{Codes} website if you are a **participant**, a **mentor** or an **organizer (admin)**. The site handles registration, teams, mentor matching and published results. Your code goes on GitHub, team chat happens on Discord and judging happens in person.

## Getting started

### Pages anyone can see

| Menu item       | What you find there                                                                        |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **REGULATIONS** | The rulebook in question-and-answer form                                                   |
| **SCHEDULE**    | The event timeline                                                                         |
| **MENTORS**     | Mentor profiles grouped by company (shows "MENTORS COMING SOON" until the roster is final) |
| **TEAMS**       | Teams that hold one of the 18 event slots                                                  |
| **RANKINGS**    | The live leaderboard once results are published, a placeholder before that                 |

On a phone, open the menu with the **☰** button in the top corner.

### Reporting a problem

Every page has a **🐛** button. Click it, fill in **What were you doing?** and **What happened instead?**, then click **SEND REPORT**. The organizers receive it right away.

## For participants

### 1. Create your account

Registration is open only during the registration window. When it is closed the page shows **REGISTRATION CLOSED**.

1. Click **REGISTER** in the top menu.
2. Fill in your name, email, a password of at least 12 characters, phone number, faculty number, course year, university and specialty.
3. If your university or specialty is not in the list, choose **Other (add your own)** and type it in.
4. Click **REGISTER**.

You are logged in and taken to your team page. Each email and faculty number can only be used once.

If many people register at once, the button counts down and a note below it says why (**RETRYING… 12S**). Keep the page open: it tries again on its own, and so does **SIGN IN** on the login page. Changing a field stops the countdown, so click the button again once you're done. If it still can't get through, it shows "Too many requests" or "The server is busy" and you can click again.

We also email you a link to verify your address. Open it within 24 hours; the **VERIFY EMAIL** page confirms it worked. If the link expired or never arrived, open **PROFILE** and click **RESEND VERIFICATION EMAIL**. When the organizers require verification, you cannot create, join or apply to a team until your email is verified, and the team page reminds you.

### 2. Get onto a team

Teams have 3 to 6 members. The team page offers three ways in:

<!-- screenshot: the NO TEAM YET panel with three buttons -->

**Create a team.** Click **CREATE TEAM**, enter a team name and click **CREATE TEAM**. You become the team's **captain** and receive a join code to share with your teammates.

**Join with a code.** Click **JOIN WITH CODE**, enter the code your captain sent you and click **JOIN TEAM**. You join immediately, no approval needed.

**Apply to a team.** Click **BROWSE TEAMS** to see teams with free spots, then click **APPLY TO JOIN**. The captain gets an email and decides. Your applications and their status appear under **MY APPLICATIONS** on the team page. Click **WITHDRAW** to cancel one that is still pending. Once you are on a team (a captain accepted you, you joined with a code or you created one), your other pending applications close by themselves. **MY APPLICATIONS** only shows while you have no team, so you see the result if you leave: the closed ones read **INVALIDATED** and one to the team you joined with its code reads **ACCEPTED**. If a team you applied to is disbanded, your application to it disappears.

You can also apply from the public **TEAMS** page when you do not have a team yet.

### 3. Get your team registered

- With fewer than 3 members your team shows **PENDING** and the message "Your team needs at least 3 members to be eligible for the hackathon."
- The moment you reach 3 members the team becomes **ACTIVE**.
- The first 18 active teams are **REGISTERED** for the event automatically. You will see "Your team is registered for the hackathon."
- If all 18 slots are taken, your team joins the waiting queue and shows its position. You are registered automatically as soon as a slot opens.
- If a registered team drops below 3 members it loses its slot, so keep your roster at 3 or more.

<!-- screenshot: team page with REGISTERED badge and members list -->

### 4. Manage your team (captains)

From **MY TEAM**:

- **JOIN CODE** shows your code to share.
- **JOIN REQUESTS** lists people who applied. Click **ACCEPT** or **DECLINE**.
- **MEMBERS** lets you remove a member.
- **DANGER ZONE → DISBAND TEAM** permanently deletes the team and releases everyone.

As a regular member, **DANGER ZONE → LEAVE TEAM** takes you off the team. If the captain leaves, the whole team is disbanded.

### 5. Choose your mentors (captains)

Once your team is registered (it holds one of the 18 event slots), the **MENTORS** panel on your team page shows a **CHOOSE MENTORS** button. A team in the queue sees a note instead, since only registered teams take part in mentor matching.

1. Click **CHOOSE MENTORS**.
2. Pick exactly 3 mentors from the cards (**STEP 1**) and click **NEXT: RANK MENTORS**.
3. Drag them into order (**STEP 2**), with your first choice at the top. You can also use Space and the arrow keys.
4. Click **SUBMIT PREFERENCES**.

The page switches to **SUBMISSION ACCEPTED** with "Preferences saved successfully." and your ranking in order, and shows it again whenever you come back. Until the organizers confirm the matching you can click **UPDATE PREFERENCES** to reopen both steps with your saved ranking filled in (a mentor who has left the event drops out, so pick a replacement); the last button then reads **UPDATE PREFERENCES** and the panel comes back with the new ranking and "Preferences updated." **CANCEL** on the first step keeps your saved ranking. If a submission fails, the form stays open with the error above it. Back on the team page, the **MENTORS** panel lists the ranking with "Preferences submitted. Waiting for matching." for the whole team; while submissions are open and the matching isn't confirmed, you also get an **UPDATE PREFERENCES** button there that brings you back to this page.

Rankings can only be submitted while the organizers have preference submission open. While it is closed, the **MENTORS** panel says so instead of showing **CHOOSE MENTORS**, and **CHOOSE YOUR MENTORS** shows your saved ranking without **UPDATE PREFERENCES**, or **SUBMISSIONS CLOSED** if your team never submitted. If it closes while you are editing, the page drops back to your saved ranking. Open pages follow a change within 30 seconds.

<!-- screenshot: drag-to-rank step -->

### 6. See your mentors

After the organizers confirm the matching, the **MENTORS** panel lists your assigned mentors, each with their photo, name and company. Every team gets 2 or 3 mentors. The matching tries to give you mentors you ranked, but a team can also receive mentors it did not pick so that nobody is left without help.

### 7. Results

After judging, the organizers publish scores. **RANKINGS** shows the top 3 on a podium and everyone else below, and refreshes every 30 seconds.

### Your profile

**PROFILE** shows your details and team (a captain also sees the join code), and whether your email is **VERIFIED** or **NOT VERIFIED**. Use **CHANGE PASSWORD** to set a new password; you stay logged in on this device and every other device is signed out.

### Forgot your password

On the login page select **PARTICIPANT**, click **FORGOT PASSWORD?**, enter your email and click **SEND RECOVERY EMAIL**. Open the link in the email within one hour, choose a new password and click **SET NEW PASSWORD**.

## For mentors

Your account is created by the organizers. They will tell you your login email and initial password.

### Log in

1. Click **LOGIN**.
2. Select **MENTOR** (on a phone, pick it from the **SIGN IN AS** dropdown).
3. Enter your email and password and click **SIGN IN**.

You land on the **MENTOR DASHBOARD** (**DASHBOARD** in the menu). Change your initial password right away under **PROFILE → CHANGE PASSWORD**. If you forget it, use **FORGOT PASSWORD?** on the login page with **MENTOR** selected, the same way as participants.

### Rank the teams you would like to mentor

Only registered teams can be ranked, so this list fills up as teams register.

1. Pick exactly 3 teams (**STEP 1**) and click **NEXT: RANK TEAMS**.
2. Drag them into order with your favourite at the top (**STEP 2**).
3. Click **SUBMIT PREFERENCES**.

The dashboard switches to **SUBMISSION ACCEPTED** with "Preferences saved successfully." and your ranking in order, and shows it again whenever you come back. Until the organizers confirm the matching you can click **UPDATE PREFERENCES** to reopen both steps with your saved ranking filled in (a team that has lost its slot drops out, so pick a replacement); the last button then reads **UPDATE PREFERENCES** and the panel comes back with the new ranking and "Preferences updated." **CANCEL** on the first step keeps your saved ranking. If a submission fails, the form stays open with the error above it.

This works only while the organizers have preference submission open. While it is closed, the dashboard shows your saved ranking without **UPDATE PREFERENCES**, or **SUBMISSIONS CLOSED** if you never submitted. If it closes while you are editing, the dashboard drops back to your saved ranking.

<!-- screenshot: mentor dashboard team selection -->

### See your assigned teams

After the matching is confirmed the dashboard shows **ASSIGNED TEAMS**. You may be assigned up to 3 teams.

### Your public profile

Your name, company, bio, technologies, availability and photo appear on the public **MENTORS** page. To change any of these, contact the organizers.

## For organizers (admin)

Log in with **ADMIN** selected. Click **ADMIN** in the menu to open **MISSION CONTROL**. The top row shows registered teams, queue length, how many teams and mentors have submitted preferences and the matching state.

<!-- screenshot: Mission Control KPI row and tabs -->

### Site tab

Opens and closes participant registration and preference submission, publishes the rankings and shows or hides the mentor roster for everyone, straight away, with no help from whoever runs the server. Each switch has its own panel showing whether it is on, when it last changed and any pending schedule.

| Panel                        | On                                                              | Off                                                               |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **PARTICIPANT REGISTRATION** | OPEN: sign-up and the REGISTER links work                       | CLOSED: the registration page says it is closed                   |
| **PREFERENCE SUBMISSIONS**   | OPEN: captains and mentors can submit and change their rankings | CLOSED: saved rankings are read-only and the API refuses new ones |
| **LIVE RANKINGS**            | LIVE: the leaderboard and team scores are public                | HIDDEN: only you see the scores                                   |
| **MENTOR ROSTER**            | COMING SOON: the MENTORS page shows the placeholder             | PUBLIC: the MENTORS page shows every mentor                       |

- **OPEN NOW**, **CLOSE NOW** (and the matching button on the other panels) switches immediately after you confirm. Visitors with the site already open see the change within 30 seconds.
- To have it happen by itself, pick a date and time in your own time zone and click **SCHEDULE**, then confirm. The server makes the change on the first visit to the site after that minute (open pages check every 30 seconds), so anyone looking sees it on time, even if nobody has Mission Control open. Each panel holds one schedule: a new one replaces it, and **CANCEL SCHEDULE** drops it. To open registration at one time and close it at another, schedule the opening, then schedule the closing once registration is open.
- Switching by hand does not cancel a schedule. If the schedule later asks for the state the site is already in, it is dropped quietly.
- Every change, by hand or scheduled, is posted to the event feed in Slack (`#event-feed-prod`; staging posts to `#event-feed-staging`).

### Registration tab

- **REGISTRATION PROGRESS** shows how many of the 18 slots are used.
- **REGISTERED** lists registered teams. **UNREGISTER** removes a team from its slot. This does not pull the next team in from the queue; do that yourself with **REGISTER**.
- **QUEUE** lists waiting teams in order. **REGISTER** gives a team a free slot.
- **UNREGISTERED** appears when an active team has neither a slot nor a queue place, for example after you unregister it. **REGISTER** there gives it a free slot again.

### Preferences tab

Shows which teams and mentors have submitted their rankings (✓ or —). Use it to chase missing submissions before running the matching.

### Matching tab

1. Close **PREFERENCE SUBMISSIONS** on the [Site tab](#site-tab) at the deadline, so the matching runs on a fixed set of rankings. A ranking still being saved at that moment either lands before the close or is refused, never after it. While submissions are open the **RUN MATCHING** dialog warns that rankings can still change after the run, but it still lets you run one, for example as a test before the deadline. If a registered team leaves or disbands after the close, the mentors who ranked it are left with 2 picks, and a team promoted from the queue has none. Check the [Preferences tab](#preferences-tab), and to let them rank, open submissions again for a short announced window before the final run.
2. Click **RUN MATCHING** and confirm. The results appear as team → mentor pairs. Every team gets up to 3 mentors, aiming for 2 or 3; the run still works if some preferences are missing. When there aren't enough mentors for 3 each, they are shared out evenly so every team gets at least 2 (that takes at least 12 active mentors for 18 teams). If the run has warnings (teams or mentors that never ranked, teams left under 3 mentors, teams left under 2), a warning toast lists them; read it before you confirm.
3. Review the pairs. You can run the matching again at any time; each run replaces the previous results.
4. Click **CONFIRM** to publish. Teams and mentors now see their assignments instead of their preferences.

Note: although the confirm dialog says it cannot be undone, running the matching again un-publishes the results until you confirm again.

### Following matching in Slack

The event feed (`#event-feed-prod`; staging posts to `#event-feed-staging`) follows the matching phase as it happens, so you don't have to keep refreshing Mission Control. It says that someone submitted, never what they chose: no rankings, ranks or team-mentor pairs.

| When                                    | The feed shows                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A team submits its first mentor ranking | "Team _X_ submitted its mentor ranking. Teams: 7 of 18 submitted."                                                                                  |
| A team changes its ranking              | "Team _X_ updated its mentor ranking."                                                                                                              |
| A mentor submits their first ranking    | "Mentor _Y_ submitted their team ranking. Mentors: 12 of 20 submitted."                                                                             |
| A mentor changes their ranking          | "Mentor _Y_ updated their team ranking."                                                                                                            |
| You run the matching                    | How many assignments went to how many teams, the run's warnings (such as teams that never submitted) and whether it cleared an earlier confirmation |
| You confirm the matching                | "Matching confirmed as final. Teams and mentors now see their assignments." (once; confirming again posts nothing)                                  |

The counts cover registered teams and active mentors, the same ones the matching uses.

### Scores tab

After judging, type each team's total score (0 to 100) and click **SAVE**. Scores stay visible only to you until rankings are published (**LIVE RANKINGS** on the [Site tab](#site-tab)); after that the public leaderboard picks up each change within 30 seconds. **SCORE OVERVIEW** charts the scores entered so far.

### Switching parts of the site on and off

Registration, preference submission, the rankings and the mentor roster are on the [Site tab](#site-tab). Only requiring participants to verify their email before forming a team is a server setting: ask whoever runs the server to change `REQUIRE_EMAIL_VERIFICATION` (see [Deployment](deployment.md#feature-flags-during-the-event)).

### Known issues

- The **EXPORT** button on the Matching tab currently shows nothing. Use the results list on screen instead.
- The admin password cannot be changed or reset from the site.
